## PART B — ARCHITECTURE

### TABLE B.3 — Minimum Room Sizes (Net Area)
**Reference:** DBC 2021, B.5.2, Table B.3  
*"The net area and clear dimension of a room/space shall be not less than the minimum sizes given in Table B.3."*

| Space | Min Net Area (m²) | Min Clear Dimension (m) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential living space (bedroom, living room) | **10.5** | **3.0** | See Figure B.23 |
| Residential studio (including bathroom and kitchen) | 21 | 3.0 | Full studio unit |
| Housekeeper's / Guard rooms (single occupancy, exclusive of toilet area) | **4.5** | **2.1** | Single occupancy only |
| Open kitchen (integrated within living space) | — | — | No separate minimum cited for open kitchen |
| Enclosed kitchen | **5.4** | **1.8** | Standalone enclosed kitchen |
| Bathroom / toilet | Refer B.8.1.5 fixture clearances | — | — |
| Toilet stalls | Refer B.8.1.4 | — | — |

**Key notes:**
- "Residential living space" covers BOTH bedroom and living room — minimum is 10.5m² net for each.
- Housekeeper's room min 4.5m² is **exclusive of toilet area** — if en-suite toilet attached, room itself must still be ≥ 4.5m².
- Kitchen minimum 5.4m² applies to **enclosed kitchen**. Open kitchens integrated into living space: no standalone minimum area specified in Table B.3.

### TABLE B.8 — Minimum Window Glazing for Daylighting
**Reference:** DBC 2021, B.6.5.2.1, Table B.8  
*"Daylighting provisions for habitable and occupiable spaces shall conform to Table B.8."*  
*"Housekeeper's rooms shall be provided with adequate daylighting similar to living spaces as shown in Table B.8."*

| Occupancy | Space | Min Glazing (% of floor area) |
|---|---|---|
| Residential and hotel | Living, bedroom spaces (excluding kitchen area) | **10%** |
| Residential and hotel | Housekeeper's rooms | **10%** (same as living) |

**Minimum lux from daylight:** 150 lux in bedrooms and living rooms (B.6.5.2.1)

**Room depth rule (B.6.5.2.2):**  
Room depth should not exceed **3× the width of the wall** in which the window is located.

## PART H — INDOOR ENVIRONMENT

---

### H.4.11 — Natural Ventilation
**Reference:** DBC 2021, H.4.11  
*"Natural ventilation of occupied spaces via windows, doors or other openings is **permitted but shall not be relied upon** to provide ventilation and thermal comfort."*

**Implication for space planning audits:**
- Natural ventilation via openable windows is **not a code requirement** — it is a permitted supplement only.
- **Mechanical HVAC is mandatory** regardless. DBC H.4.12 governs.
- A room without an openable window does **NOT fail DBC on ventilation** — it fails only if **daylighting** is absent (Table B.8).
- A room without any window DOES fail DBC B.6.5.2.1 (no daylighting) — this is the enforceable violation.

---

### H.4.12 — Mechanical Ventilation
**Reference:** DBC 2021, H.4.12.1  
All buildings shall be ventilated mechanically or by mixed mode. ASHRAE 62.1, ASHRAE 62.2 and ASHRAE 170 govern minimum outdoor air flow rates.

## VERDICT THRESHOLDS FOR REPORTS

| Verdict | Condition |
|---|---|
| **DBC Fail** | Does not meet mandatory DBC 2021 minimum. Cannot be corrected by design interior without structural/façade change. Will not pass DM authority submission. |
| **DBC Marginal** | Meets DBC minimum but falls below GCC luxury practice standard. Compliant but substandard for typology. |
| **DBC Pass** | Meets or exceeds DBC 2021 minimum AND GCC best practice. |
| **GCC Fail** | Meets DBC minimum but fails luxury market standard. Flagged as design deficiency, not authority violation. |

## COMMON ERRORS TO AVOID

| ❌ Wrong (Previous Reports) | ✅ Correct (DBC 2021) |
|---|---|
| Kitchen min 5.0m² | Kitchen min **5.4m²** (Table B.3) |
| Bedroom min 9m² | Bedroom/living min **10.5m²** (Table B.3) |
| "5% openable ventilation required" | **Not required** — HVAC mandatory (H.4.11) |
| "Maid room fails on ventilation" | Maid room fails on **daylighting** only (B.6.5.2.1) |
| Natural ventilation = code requirement | Natural ventilation = permitted supplement only (H.4.11) |
| Corridor internal unit min 1.2m | Internal unit corridor min **1.0m** (Table B.5) |
