Arabic reference series  ·  DHA / DoH guidance  ·  RIBA & ISO 19650 process

A chapter-by-chapter study of how a healthcare facility moves from medical need to an approved architectural programme — who takes part, which documents are produced, and how clinical requirements are reviewed, coordinated and turned into verifiable spatial decisions. Written in Arabic, with English and French technical terms kept as used in practice.
